About SK Transports KSA

Powering Heavy Lifting Infrastructure in Saudi Arabia

A leading heavy truck mobile crane rental and machinery sales organization serving the Kingdom's most demanding industrial, energy, and Vision 2030 projects.

Our Mission

Engineering Safety & Heavy Capacity Across the Kingdom

SK Transports KSA was established to provide uncompromised heavy lifting and machinery logistics for general contractors, engineering firms, oil & gas operators, and governmental infrastructure developers.

With strategic yards operating in Riyadh, Dammam/Jubail, Jeddah, and the NEOM corridor, we maintain a fleet of modern SANY mobile cranes ranging from 50 Ton to 500 Ton, backed by Aramco-certified operators and dedicated 24/7 mobile field service units.
