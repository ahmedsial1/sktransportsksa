Daily Protocol

The 10-Point Pre-Lift Daily Inspection Checklist

Before turning the key on any job site in Saudi Arabia, our certified crane operators and rigging supervisors execute this mandatory 10-point check:

01
Wire Rope & Drum Spooling Inspection for kinks, bird-caging, broken wires, and proper spooling.
02
Anti-Two-Block (A2B) Switches Functional test of upper limit trip switch and hoist lock mechanism.
03
LMI Computer & Radius Calibration Verification of boom length, angle transducers, and tare weight zeroing.
04
Hydraulic Outrigger Full Extension & Matting Level bubble check and deployment of hardwood/steel load distribution mats.
05
Hydraulic Cylinder & Seal Pressure Visual check for fluid leaks, holding valve integrity, and cylinder creep.
06
Main Hook Block & Safety Latch Hook throat opening measurement, bearing rotation, and spring latch check.
07
Wind Speed Anemometer Reading Boom tip wind monitoring against maximum allowable operational limits.
08
Ground Bearing Capacity & Underground Hazards Site survey verification for buried pipes, trenches, and soil density.
09
Emergency Stop & Cabin Controls Pilot controls, deadman switch, horn, fire extinguisher, and mirrors.
10
Pre-Lift Tool-Box Talk (TBT) & Permit to Work Review of Critical Lift Plan with riggers, signalmen, and HSE engineers.
