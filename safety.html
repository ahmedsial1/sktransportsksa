Safety & Compliance

Zero-Compromise Safety: Saudi Aramco GI Standards, TUV Middle East Certification, and Rigorous Daily Pre-Lift Inspections.

Saudi Aramco GI 7.027 & 7.028

Our entire fleet undergoes strict Aramco mechanical testing and holds valid inspection stickers for oil & gas refinery operations.

  • Aramco valid crane inspection sticker
  • Aramco-tested heavy crane operators
  • Certified Rigger I & II on site

TUV Middle East 3rd Party Certs

Every lifting gear, wire rope sling, shackle, and spreader beam is certified with valid 3rd-party proof load test certificates.

  • Annual comprehensive NDT testing
  • Load Moment Indicator (LMI) calibration
  • Rigging gear color coding compliant

10-Point Pre-Lift Daily Protocol

Mandatory daily safety checklist performed prior to extending outriggers or starting engine ignition.

  • Ground compaction & matting check
  • Anti-Two-Block (A2B) switch test
  • Hydraulic pressure & seal inspection
