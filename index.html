Uncompromising Standards

Aramco & TUV Certified Safety Standards

Safety is our first priority. Every crane and operator undergoes stringent third-party certification.

  • Saudi Aramco GI 7.027 & 7.028 Compliance

    All cranes meet Aramco safety inspection sticker protocols with calibrated load indicators (LMI).

  • TUV Middle East Third-Party Certification

    Annual and semi-annual structural and hydraulic non-destructive testing (NDT) and proof load tests.

  • 10-Point Daily Pre-Lift Inspection

    Comprehensive daily checks on wire ropes, outriggers, hydraulic pressure, limit switches, and ground conditions.

  • Level 1, 2 & 3 Certified Riggers & Operators

    Trained operators with extensive experience in petrochemical, civil infrastructure, and refinery operations.
